**What & why**
Near Miss Office Comments showed the vessel-review blocker even after the record was already in `OFFICE_COMMENTS_COMPLETED`, and the workspace did not show the saved office comment. This change exposes the saved office-comment text in the Near Miss detail payload and renders completed vessel/office review notes read-only.

**Tier + triggers**
Tier 2 - small behavior/API response change. Trigger: Near Miss detail endpoint response adds an `office_comment` field, and the existing Office Comments screen gains a completed-state display.

**Domains touched**
Step 1 Safety Near Miss workflow and Safety API display contract.

**Decisions**
Accepted default: Office comments are read from the latest completed Office Comments phase log when `vims_safety_incident.office_comment` is empty, because current Near Miss acceptance records comments in phase history.
