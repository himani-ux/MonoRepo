# CR-005 - Injury PDF Title

## What & why

When an incident has a saved Phase 1 injury record, the exported PDF must identify itself as an `Injury Report`. Incidents without a saved injury record continue to print as `Incident Report`. This keeps the final report title aligned with the user's `Record injury` choice without adding another database flag.

## Tier + triggers

Tier 2 - small behavior change. Trigger: changed PDF output contract for the incident export route.

## Domains touched

Domains 2 and 8: user-facing report output/state and PDF/export behavior.

## Decisions

No new SSOT decision ID assigned. CR-005 clarifies FEAT-SAF-PDF-001 behavior: the incident PDF title is derived from the existing injury record relation.
