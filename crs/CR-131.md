**What & why**
Near Miss PDFs rendered Vessel review comment and Office comments inside a two-column table. Long office comments could look cramped and could be cut off at the page edge. This change renders those comments as full-width paragraph blocks that preserve line breaks and can continue naturally across pages.

**Tier + triggers**
Tier 2 - small behavior change. Trigger: Near Miss PDF output layout changes for review comments.

**Domains touched**
Step 1 Safety Near Miss PDF workflow/display.

**Decisions**
Accepted default: Vessel review comment and Office comments remain under the existing Review Comments heading, but each comment is rendered as a full-width labelled block instead of a label/value table row.
