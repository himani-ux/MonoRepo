**Doc cascade** - `certs_docsuite/APP_FLOW.md` updated approval queue and detail permissions. `certs_docsuite/BACKEND_STRUCTURE.md` updated approve/reject endpoint role contract. `certs_docsuite/USER_GUIDE.md` updated DPA/PIC responsibility.
