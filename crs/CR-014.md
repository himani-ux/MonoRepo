**What & why** - SCM meeting hosting must be blocked until the vessel is ready for WRH review: ship time must be configured and every crew member required for SCM readiness must have available WRH data. This prevents users from hosting an SCM meeting while the same screen still shows a WRH warning.

**Tier + triggers** - Tier 3. Triggers: changed validation rule for SCM meeting hosting and contradiction of existing canonical statements that WRH gaps are warn-only and do not block SCM meeting creation.

**Domains touched** - Step 1 domains affected: SCM meeting workflow, vessel WRH readiness, ship-time configuration, validation/error handling.

**Decisions** - New SSOT decision `D-MAINT-CR014`: SCM Regular and Ad-Hoc meeting creation is allowed only when WRH readiness is clear for the vessel/date: ship-time configuration exists and all roster crew included in SCM readiness have available, compliant WRH data. This supersedes `D-GAP-M11` for SCM meeting creation only; existing created-meeting detail, PDF, and Office Comment closure warnings remain informational unless explicitly changed later. Accepted default CR-014-D1: backend validation is authoritative and blocks SCM hosting when any WRH readiness warning condition is active. Accepted default CR-014-D2: the frontend surfaces WRH host readiness near the host action and blocks obvious current-date warnings, while submit still rechecks server-side when the selected date changes.
