**What & why** - The Audit Detail scorecard screen can display more than the required 14 inspection areas after save, including seeded/demo rows, which makes the populated counter read incorrectly and clutters the saved state. This change keeps the Audit scorecard UI focused on the required 14-area summary and preserves editability after rows are saved.

**Tier + triggers** - Tier 2. Triggers: changed Audit Detail screen behavior/state and frontend-visible scorecard presentation. No endpoint, schema, dependency, role permission, or analytics change is introduced.

**Domains touched** - Step 1 domains: Audit Detail UI; scorecard submit readiness display; Audit frontend regression coverage; Audit user/developer documentation.

**Decisions** - D-MAINT-CR178: accepted default - treat the live scorecard screen as a fixed 14-area operational summary; hide non-required/demo `master_audit_area` rows from the editable grid and calculate the populated count from the displayed 14 rows only. Saved rows remain editable through the same controls and Save Scorecard mutation. User-corrected UI direction: retain the existing scorecard row styling; cleanup means data/payload cleanup, not a visual redesign.
