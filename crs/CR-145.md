**What & why** - Audit Lead Auditor, Conductor, and HoD authority should come from the audit record and active Audit master assignments instead of permanent profile-wide grants. This keeps one user from receiving Lead Auditor, Conductor, or HoD actions across every audit just because a profile label or designation matches those words.

**Tier + triggers** - Tier 3. Triggers: changed role permission behavior, Audit-CAR workflow authorization changes, frontend route/action permission behavior changes, and a cross-module boundary with the shared CAR workflow engine.

**Domains touched** - Step 1 domains: Audit permissions, Audit registration/detail/checklist/finding/closure workflow, Audit-CAR workflow, frontend Audit navigation/action gating, Audit documentation, and tests.

**Decisions** - D-MAINT-CR145: Use `AuditDetail.conductor_user_id`, `AuditDetail.lead_auditor_user_id`, and active `MasterHodAssignment` rows as the source of assignment-scoped permissions. Do not create fake `msc_profiles` rows for Lead Auditor, Conductor, or HoD.

D-MAINT-CR145A: Keep global `msc_profiles.process_ids` for stable office/vessel roles such as SEQ/DPA/Fleet Manager/Master/Office Superintendent. Assignment-scoped gates are added only after the target audit record is known.
