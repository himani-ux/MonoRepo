**What & why**

Incident PDF Evidence (Documents) should stop printing the legacy "Evidence notes" block. Users only want saved attachments/documents and witness notes in the Evidence section; internal notes such as "Initial root cause entry" should not appear in the formal PDF.

**Tier + triggers**

Tier 2 - small behavior/output contract change. Trigger: changed incident PDF layout/content contract.

**Domains touched**

Step 1 domains touched: Safety incident Phase 4 evidence presentation, incident PDF export.

Mini-interrogation:
- Should evidence upload/storage change? No. Existing evidence rows and metadata remain unchanged.
- Should document attachments still print? Yes. Attachment document blocks remain in Evidence (Documents).
- Should witness notes still print? Yes. Witness Notes remain in Evidence (Documents).
- Should legacy evidence-note-only rows print? No. Evidence notes without attachments are hidden from the incident PDF.
- Should PDF section selection behavior change? No. Evidence still prints only when `evidence_documents` is included.

**Decisions**

D-MAINT-CR030 records the current evidence PDF presentation rule.
