**What & why** - Near Miss records currently keep the vessel draft reference after they are submitted to office. This change implements the approved technical plan so a Near Miss keeps `DRAFT-{VslCode}/{YYYY}/T{nnn}` while under vessel-side review, then receives the formal `{VslCode}/{YYYY}/{NNN}` number when it enters `READY_FOR_OFFICE_COMMENTS`.

**Tier + triggers** - Tier 2. Trigger: changed business behavior for generated Near Miss numbers and the Near Miss submit/resubmit workflow.

**Domains touched** - Step 1 domains: Near Miss reporting workflow; HOD/Master submit-to-office review path; incident/near-miss numbering decision D-GAP-C1; Safety API display contract; Safety register/detail/PDF consumers of `incident_number`.

**Decisions** - D-MAINT-CR155: Near Miss uses the same persisted `vims_safety_incident.incident_number` column as Incidents. Vessel-side drafts keep `DRAFT-{VslCode}/{YYYY}/T{nnn}`. On transition to `READY_FOR_OFFICE_COMMENTS`, Near Miss receives a formal number generated by the existing formal allocator, removing both `DRAFT-` and the `T` marker. The operation is idempotent: already-formal numbers are not reallocated.

**Doc cascade** - `safety_ssot/VIMS-SAFETY-MODULE-SSOT.md` records D-MAINT-CR155 and the Near Miss numbering timing. `safety_docsuite/PRD.md` states that office-submitted Near Misses no longer display draft references. `safety_docsuite/APP_FLOW.md` states that submit/resubmit to office assigns the formal Near Miss number in the same transaction. `safety_docsuite/BACKEND_STRUCTURE.md` documents that `incident_number` remains the only persisted number column and no schema change is required. `safety_docsuite/VALIDATION_RULES.md` adds the final-number validation and idempotency rule. `safety_docsuite/USER_GUIDE.md` explains draft versus official Near Miss numbers. `Docs/NEAR_MISS_DRAFT_PREFIX_FORMAL_NUMBER_PLAN.md` is updated from plan-only to implemented-local status. `safety_docsuite/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` unchanged because this change does not add schema, migration, external integration, or data backfill. Historical logs, old notifications, and already generated PDFs unchanged because the plan explicitly excludes cosmetic history rewrites.

**Tests** - Added focused regression coverage in `psc-backend/tests/safety/test_incident_numbering.py`, `psc-backend/tests/safety/test_near_miss_create_any_rank.py`, and `psc-backend/tests/safety/test_nm_review_workflow.py`. Verified: vessel-created Near Miss stays draft while pending vessel review; direct Master create formalizes immediately; Master submit-to-office formalizes; HOD review keeps draft; crew rework keeps draft; Master rework formalizes; already formal numbers are not reallocated.

**Fidelity check** - no deltas.

**Exemptions** - none.
