# CR-042 - Remove Lessons Learned phase and rename Office Check to Office Review

## What & why
The current incident workflow should no longer include a separate Lessons Learned phase. The office review step should be labelled Office Review instead of Office Check and must let office users enter an unrestricted office comment directly on that screen. This keeps the workflow shorter and makes the office decision screen capture the key office review note instead of hiding it elsewhere.

## Tier + triggers
Tier 3 - structural. Triggers: removes a visible incident workflow phase that is currently documented in the SSOT and IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN, changes visible phase labels/navigation, adds a new Office Review screen state/input, adds nullable `vims_safety_incident.office_comment` through a migration, and changes the Phase 7 office-review payload/response contract.

## Domains touched
Step 1 Domains 2, 5, 6, 7, and 8:
- Domain 2: incident phase map, visible workflow tabs, Office Review screen state, and routing after action capture.
- Domain 5: workflow validation/transition expectations after Lessons Learned is removed, and office comment persistence on review.
- Domain 6: incident data model/migration for `office_comment`.
- Domain 7: API/PDF field contract for Office Review comments.
- Domain 8: frontend/backend regression coverage for the shortened workflow and office comment save.

## Decisions
- D-MAINT-CR042 accepted default: Lessons Learned is removed from the current visible incident workflow and user navigation; legacy data/API compatibility remains where required for old records.
- D-MAINT-CR042 accepted default: Preventive Action continues directly to Office Review.
- D-MAINT-CR042 accepted default: Office Check is renamed Office Review in visible labels, phase tabs, docs, and tests.
- D-MAINT-CR042 accepted default: Office Review exposes an Office Comments textarea with no frontend word/character limit and persists it through the existing or minimal office-review save contract.
