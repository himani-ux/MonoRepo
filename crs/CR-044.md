# CR-044 - Allow PIC and DPA Office Review decisions for every incident risk band

## What & why
Incident Office Review decisions should not depend on whether the incident risk band is GREEN, YELLOW, or RED. A PIC or DPA user may accept, close, or send an incident back for rework for any risk band. This replaces the earlier risk-band closer model so office users do not get blocked by risk-band-specific authority rules during Office Review.

## Tier + triggers
Tier 3 - structural. Triggers: changes documented role permission behavior for incident Office Review decisions, changes backend validation/permission enforcement, changes frontend Office Review action availability, and supersedes existing canonical statements that tied incident closure authority to risk band.

## Domains touched
Step 1 Domains 2, 5, 7, and 8:
- Domain 2: Office Review screen state and user-facing role/action wording.
- Domain 5: office decision validation and risk-band-independent role permission rules.
- Domain 7: Office Review preflight/accept/rework API behavior and response wording.
- Domain 8: backend/frontend regression coverage for PIC and DPA actions across risk bands.

## Decisions
- D-MAINT-CR044 supersedes the older risk-band closure authority statements for the current implemented Incident Office Review workflow: PIC and DPA may accept, close, or send to rework regardless of GREEN/YELLOW/RED risk band.
- D-MAINT-CR044 accepted default: Fleet Manager-specific RED closure is no longer required by the current implemented Office Review path unless a later CR restores that separately.
