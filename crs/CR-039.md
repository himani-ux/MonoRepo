# CR-039 - Keep incident phases 2 through 6 editable

## What & why
Incident phases 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 must stay editable for authorized users so investigation details, actions, lessons, and evidence can be corrected without forcing users into a one-way workflow. This keeps the editable investigation workspace flexible while preserving approval/closure locks.

## Tier + triggers
Tier 2 - small behavior change. Triggers: changed Incident screen editability state and workflow behavior. No database, endpoint, dependency, or role model change is planned.

## Domains touched
Step 1 Domains 2, 4, 5, and 8:
- Domain 2: Incident phase screen states.
- Domain 4: Incident workflow/edit window.
- Domain 5: validation/edit locks.
- Domain 8: regression tests for phase editability.

## Decisions
- D-MAINT-CR039 accepted default: phases 2 through 6 remain editable for authorized users until the incident is office-approved, closed, or superseded.
- D-MAINT-CR039 accepted default: keeping a phase editable does not skip formal transition validation; submit/continue actions still use the existing workflow gates.
