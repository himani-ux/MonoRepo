**What & why** - Office comments in the Incident PDF should display as the user typed them. The full-width comment block must not split a long office comment into artificial chunks; it should pass the stored text as one paragraph and preserve typed line breaks and repeated spaces rather than adding arbitrary breaks.

**Tier + triggers** - Tier 2; the generated Incident PDF contract changes.
