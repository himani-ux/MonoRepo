# CR-019 - Simplify Final Record History UI

## What & why

The Incident Final Record page should be easier for vessel users to read. The user-facing Change History card and History Rows metric are removed from the current Final Record UI, Phase History is restyled as a plain sequence of completed workflow steps, and the risk card must not show `NOT_APPLICABLE` as if it were a risk level or useful user-facing classifier.

## Tier + triggers

Tier 3 - structural Maintain Mode change. Triggers: changed Final Record screen state and contradiction of canonical SSOT / implementation-plan statements that describe Final Record as exposing change history or a full audit trail panel.

## Domains touched

Step 1 domains to verify: Incident Final Record UI, final-record audit presentation, phase history wording, risk/IMO classifier display, frontend regression tests, and canonical safety documentation.

## Decisions

New SSOT decision `D-MAINT-CR019`: the current user-facing Incident Final Record page shows a simplified final summary, approvals, reports, and an easy-to-read Phase History timeline. It does not show the Change History card or History Rows metric. Field/change-history data remains available through backend audit APIs for authorized audit/export use, but it is not a routine Final Record UI card. The UI formats `NOT_APPLICABLE` classifier values as `No IMO class` instead of exposing the raw enum.
