**Tier + triggers** - Tier 3 structural maintain change. Triggers: current screen behavior changes, current PDF contract changes, and canonical docs that previously described an injury investigation narrative are superseded while the database column remains legacy-compatible.

**Domains touched** - Step 1 domains: incident Phase 1 injury reporting, Incident PDF output, validation/documentation, and safety backend compatibility.

**Decisions** - D-MAINT-CR078: the incident-level **Describe What happened?** field is the only current user-facing narrative for incident and injury reports. `what_happened_narrative` remains a nullable legacy storage/API field but is not shown in the current injury form and is not printed in the current Incident PDF.
