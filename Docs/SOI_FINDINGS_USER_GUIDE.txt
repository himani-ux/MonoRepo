SOI FINDINGS USER GUIDE

This guide is for vessel users who record and close Safety Officer Inspection
findings in VIMS.

SOI means Safety Officer Inspection.
Finding means a safety problem found during the SOI round.


1. Who Uses SOI Findings

Safety Officer:
Creates SOI findings after the paper SOI checklist is completed.

Chief Officer:
Usually acts as Safety Officer.

Second Engineer:
Can act as alternate Safety Officer only when assigned in the system.

Master:
Reviews and closes SOI findings after the Safety Officer marks them ready for
closure.

DPA:
Can reopen a closed SOI finding if follow-up evidence shows the closure is not
acceptable.


2. Before Adding a Finding

Do these steps first:

1. Create the SOI record.
2. Select the SOI areas.
3. Download the SOI paper checklist.
4. Print the checklist.
5. Complete the inspection on board.
6. Write the findings on the paper checklist.
7. Keep the signed paper checklist safely on board.

Important:
SOI is paper-first. The paper checklist is the main inspection record.
The system stores only the formal findings and closure details.
Do not scan or upload the paper checklist.


3. How To Open SOI Findings

Open VIMS.
Go to Safety.
Open Safety Officer Inspection.
Open the required SOI record.
Open Findings.

You will see the SOI Findings Register.

If the paper checklist has not been downloaded yet, use Download paper first.
The Add Finding button appears only after the paper checklist is generated.


4. How To Add A Finding

Click Add Finding.

Fill the fields carefully.

Area:
Select the area where the problem was found.
Only areas selected for this SOI can be used.

Checklist item:
Select the checklist item related to the problem.
This helps connect the finding to the paper checklist item.

Severity:
Select how serious the problem is.

LOW means small issue with low safety impact.
MED means important issue that needs action.
HIGH means serious issue or strong safety risk.

Priority:
Select how quickly the action should be handled.

LOW means routine action.
MED means action should be followed up soon.
HIGH means action should be treated urgently.

Root cause:
Select the main reason for the problem.
Use the closest available option.

Category tag:
Select the type of finding, such as Safety, Security, Environment, MLC,
Training, Operational, Management, or Others.

Person Responsible:
Select the person who will make sure the action is completed.
This is responsibility for follow-up, not blame.
If no person is selected, it defaults to the Safety Officer.

Title:
Write a short name for the finding.

Good title:
Emergency marker faded

Bad title:
Problem

Description:
Write clearly what was seen, where it was seen, and why it matters.

Good description:
Emergency escape marker near the bridge exit is faded and not clearly visible
in low light.

Bad description:
Marker issue.

Photo evidence:
Attach a photo when useful.
For HIGH severity, photo evidence is required.
Allowed photo types are JPG, JPEG, or PNG.
Maximum photo size is 3 MB.

Click Save finding.


5. Important Save Rules

The SOI paper checklist must be downloaded before findings can be saved.

The checklist unique ID must match the ID printed on the paper checklist.

The selected area must be one of the areas selected for this SOI.

HIGH severity findings require a photo.

If the finding looks incident-worthy, the system may ask whether to create an
Incident or keep it in SOI only.

If you keep a HIGH severity item in SOI only, write a clear reason.

If the text suggests a life-threatening issue, the system will require
escalation through Incident or Near Miss before saving can continue.


6. After Saving A Finding

The finding appears in the SOI Findings Register.

New findings start as Open.

Check that the title, severity, priority, responsible person, due date, and
photo evidence are correct.

If there are more paper findings for the same SOI area, add them one by one.


7. Submitting An SOI Area

After all findings for an area are entered in VIMS, submit that area.

In the SOI Findings Register, look for the pending area card.
Click Submit Area.

Submit an area only after all paper findings for that area are entered.

When all selected areas are submitted, the SOI can move toward Master closure.


8. Closing An SOI Finding

The normal closure flow is:

1. Safety Officer confirms the action is completed.
2. Safety Officer opens the finding closure page.
3. Safety Officer enters typed name and closure note.
4. Safety Officer clicks Mark pending closure.
5. Master reviews the finding.
6. Master enters typed name and closure note.
7. Master clicks Approve closure.
8. The finding becomes Closed.

If Master is not satisfied, Master clicks Reject to Open and enters a reason.
The finding returns to Open and must be corrected again.


9. Finding Status Meaning

Open:
The finding is active and action is still required.

Carried forward:
The finding is still open from an earlier SOI cycle.

Pending Closure:
Safety Officer has signed the handoff and the finding is waiting for Master
review.

Master Approved:
Master approval has been recorded during the closure process.

Closed:
The finding closure is complete.


10. Paper And Digital Signature Rule

Safety Officer and Assistant signatures remain on the paper SOI checklist.

Master closure approval is recorded digitally in VIMS.

The digital signature records typed name, time, and device reference.


11. Repeat Findings

If the same or similar issue appears again, VIMS may show a repeat-finding
badge.

Do not ignore repeat findings.
Repeat findings should be reviewed carefully because they may show that the
previous corrective action was not strong enough.


12. Common Problems

Add Finding button not visible:
The paper checklist may not be downloaded yet, the SOI may be closed, or your
login may not have the required permission.

Finding not saving:
Check the checklist unique ID, selected area, required fields, and photo
requirement for HIGH severity.

HIGH severity not saving:
Upload a JPG, JPEG, or PNG photo under 3 MB.

Life-threat warning shown:
Create or link the required Incident or Near Miss before continuing.

Master closure button not visible:
The finding may not be in Pending Closure, or the logged-in user may not be the
Master.

DPA reopen button not visible:
Only DPA can reopen a closed SOI finding.


13. Simple Checklist For Users

Before saving:
Area is correct.
Checklist item is selected if available.
Title is short and clear.
Description says what, where, and why.
Severity is correct.
Priority is correct.
Responsible person is correct.
HIGH severity has photo.
Life-threatening issue is escalated.

Before submitting area:
All paper findings for that area are entered.
No finding is missing from the paper checklist.

Before closure:
Corrective action is completed.
Evidence is enough.
Safety Officer has added closure note.
Master has reviewed and approved.
